20. Scenario 5 — New Course Launch

A new course is launched.

Thousands of learners may request the course at the same time.

Large number of learners
          |
          v
    Load Balancer
          |
          v
    Course Service
          |
          v
       Cache

Frequently requested course information can be served from the cache rather than repeatedly querying the database.

If application workload increases significantly, the Course Service can also scale its containers.

This prevents a sudden increase in traffic from unnecessarily overwhelming one application instance or repeatedly hitting the database for the same information.
